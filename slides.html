How the agents work

Two gpt-6-luna agents behind one set of safety checks

SOMbot turns a buyer's request into searches and verified answers. Listing Helper turns a seller's notes into a priced draft. A small safety guard screens suspicious messages first. Tools are read-only; the AI never posts or sends.

There are two agents on gpt-6-luna. SOMbot helps buyers and the Listing Helper helps sellers. Both sit behind the same input checks and a small safety guard. Their tools are read-only code over the listings database, so the AI chooses what to search but code decides what exists and what it costs. The AI never posts or sends anything.

Safety

Eight layers, proven against real attacks

1 · Input checksinjection patterns, obfuscation, PII redaction
2 · AI safety guardsecond opinion on suspicious messages
3 · Untrusted datascam/injection listings held from the AI
4 · Least privilegeread-only tools; humans post and send
5 · Output checksreal IDs, verified prices, canary token
6 · Spend limitstoken budgets, rate and step limits
7 · Marketplace policyhonor code, no weapons/drugs/contact info
8 · App securitykey server-side, CSP, upload checks

Because members write the listings, injection and scams are real risks.
